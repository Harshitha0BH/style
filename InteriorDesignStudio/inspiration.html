A simple checklist

Before you redesign

01 · Measure firstRecord wall lengths, door swings, windows and existing furniture.
02 · Choose a feelingDecide whether the room should feel calm, lively, cosy or focused.
03 · Layer your lightCombine ceiling lights with task lamps and softer accent lighting.
04 · Edit your palettePick a base colour, a supporting shade and one accent tone.
05 · Mix texturesBring in wood, fabric, ceramics, plants or woven materials.
06 · Keep it functionalLeave clear paths and make everyday items easy to reach.
